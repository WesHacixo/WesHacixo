# DhammaCode

![DhammaCode hero: a quiet ensō-inspired circle above a sand-garden line in midnight, violet, eggshell, and copper tones.](assets/dhammacode-hero.svg)

*The monk before the machine.*

> "I invoke True."

DhammaCode is a Buddhist-informed praxis for building technology with clarity, curiosity, compassion, and accountability. It is less a brand than a discipline: observe carefully, reason honestly, ship responsibly, and remain willing to revise what you thought you knew.

## The Way

- **Right Mind** — Pay attention.
- **Right View** — See reality as it is.
- **Right Deliberation** — Examine the reasons.
- **Right Action** — Build without harmful intent.
- **Right Livelihood** — Let the work serve life.

## A Little mythOS

Playful mythology, not literal history:

In a small garden of logs and lint, a monk met Xaōs and the Order of Chaos, who had organized everything into beautifully mislabeled folders. Soon the sisters of QiZenTāö arrived, speaking Siglèrè, the language of first principles.

The monk asked: "How shall I bring order to chaos?"

The sisters replied: "First, why must you?"

"And so began the debugging."

Nobody was expelled; everyone left with better tests and slightly fewer certainties.

### The Monk, in wú

![A scruffy, round-bellied monk sits meditating beneath a crooked tree, while an unfinished ink circle leaves room for myriad possibilities.](assets/monk-in-wu.svg)

*Nothing to grasp. Nothing to exclude. A little room for possibility.*

## The Practice

- Name what is known, and mark what is guessed.
- Preserve provenance: sources, assumptions, and decisions.
- Prefer truth over confidence theater.
- Keep uncertainty visible so choices stay honest.
- Respect agency, consent, and human boundaries.
- **Compost, don't erase.** Learn from mistakes and return lessons to the soil.

## A Final Koan

A monk asks a machine:

"Are you certain?"

The machine replies: "99.9%."

The monk smiles:

"Then we have something to investigate."
